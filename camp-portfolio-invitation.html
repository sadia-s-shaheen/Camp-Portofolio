From launch day to showcase day

The number of weeks will follow the length of your break.

  • 🎒Before the break

    Launch Day

    A portfolio bootcamp: LinkedIn, GitHub, branding, how recruiters read your site, and your first version online.

  • 🤝End of launch day

    Find Your Camp

    Answer a short quiz: how active you like a camp, how you like to chat, your interests and hobbies. AI suggests people who fit. You meet them and confirm, or ask for a rematch.

  • 🛠️During the break

    Build, together

    One small mission and one short check-in a week, with your camp. Plan, design, build, write up, ship, polish.

  • 🎤After the break

    Showcase Day

    Everyone presents their live site for a few minutes, in front of everyone. The audience comments on each portfolio live and picks a top 10. The top picks get awarded.

Questions

Is this definitely happening?
No. This is an interest check. We'll decide based on how many people want it and how many mentors we can gather.
Who is it for?
Mainly second-year students heading into the break after semester three. Other years can say they're curious in the form.
How do camps get formed?
A short quiz, then AI suggests people with a similar camp style. You meet them, then privately confirm or ask for a rematch. The AI recommends, you decide, and organisers finalise the groups.
Who judges the showcase?
You do. Everyone presents, everyone leaves comments, and each person picks a top 10 (not themselves). Picks stay private until the end so popularity doesn't steer the room.
What if my web development isn't strong yet?
That's okay. Everyone picks their own stack and style. You'll have resources, peer feedback and a mentor.
How much time per week?
Not fixed yet. The form asks, and your answers will shape how big each mission is.
